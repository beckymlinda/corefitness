Our History

It started with
a simple idea.

Core Fitness was founded in Sector 5, Lilongwe by people who love training but were tired of gyms that felt cold, crowded and intimidating. The idea was simple: build a gym where anyone can walk in and feel they belong, whether it's their first day or their thousandth.

From the start, we listened to our members. Women told us they wanted a space to train comfortably, so we built a dedicated women's studio with every machine a modern gym has, plus AI-powered training tools and recovery massagers. Lifters wanted a serious floor, so we built an advanced, well-organised men's section. Members wanted more than weights, so we brought in boxing, karate, ab training, yoga and flexibility classes with trainers who care.

Today Core Fitness is a community. People come for the equipment and stay for the people.
