Our Story

Built in Lilongwe.
Built for everyone.

Core Fitness started in Sector 5, Lilongwe because too many gyms felt like places you had to already be fit to walk into. We wanted something different: a gym where a first-timer gets the same attention as a seasoned lifter, where women train in a space built for them, and where the goal is a stronger body and a clearer mind.

What began as a passion for training has grown into one of the city's most complete fitness homes, with an advanced, well-organised men's floor, a fully equipped women's studio with AI-assisted training and recovery massagers, a boxing corner, karate, ab training, and dedicated yoga and flexibility classes.
