Questions

Good to know.

I've never been to a gym. Is Core Fitness right for me?
Absolutely. Every new member gets a guided first session where a coach tours you around, learns your goals and shows you how to use the equipment safely. You'll never be left to figure it out alone.
Is the women's side really private?
Yes. The Women's Studio is a dedicated, women-only space with its own full set of modern equipment, AI-powered training tools and recovery massagers, so you can train comfortably and confidently.
How do I pay?
You can pay at the front desk in cash or by mobile money (Airtel Money / TNM Mpamba) and bank transfer. Register online first and we'll confirm everything with you on WhatsApp.
Can I cancel or freeze my membership?
Monthly plans can be cancelled anytime before your next billing date. If you're travelling or injured, talk to us about freezing your membership.
Do I need experience for boxing or karate?
Not at all. Both classes welcome total beginners. Our trainers start with the fundamentals and progress you at your own pace.
What should I bring on my first day?
Comfortable workout clothes, trainers, a towel and a water bottle. For boxing, we can lend you gloves for your first sessions.
